Time and Space Complexity

Time complexity is a measure to check how much time an algorithm might take, it will always differ based on the size of the input but time complexity can give us a good idea of how much it may take in average, worst and best case scenarios.

Space Complexity is the same but for the amount of space a algorithm might take to provide output, it includes all the vars, maps and tables created during its runtime, it is represented with O(n). together they can show us how efficient an algorthm or a piece of code must be.
